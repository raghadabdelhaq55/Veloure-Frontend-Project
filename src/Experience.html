A day arranged
down to the minute.

The first Veloure motorcar was a 1963 Silver Cloud, kept in a Monaco garage by a man who lent it only to friends, and only after dinner. He believed a car should be introduced, not handed over — that the person driving ought to know what they were holding.

Twelve years later, that is still the whole method. We keep a small register rather than a large fleet, we photograph each motorcar the morning it leaves, and we send a specialist rather than a clerk. Nothing about the process is automated, which is why it takes twenty minutes and never longer.

“A great motorcar should be introduced, never merely handed over.”

Émile Veloure, 1963
