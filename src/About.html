———— Since MMXIV

A small house,
kept deliberately small

Veloure began with one motorcar and a rule: never lend a car you would not drive yourself, and never lend it to someone you have not met. We have grown to thirty-eight motorcars across four cities, and the rule has not moved.

We own every car in the register outright. There is no broker between you and the keys, no third-party garage, no partner fleet quietly filling a gap. When a motorcar is not right, it leaves the register and does not return.

Nineteen chauffeurs, eleven technicians, four ateliers, one standard. Most of our guests come back within the year, and a third of them ask for the same driver — which we take as the only review that matters.
